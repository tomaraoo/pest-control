Common pest warning signs

Small signs can point to a much larger infestation.

Pest activity often starts out of sight. Early inspection helps stop termites, cockroaches, rodents, and other pests before damage and health risks spread.

Termite activity

Hollow wood and mud tubes may signal hidden structural damage.

Termites can remain concealed inside walls and timber while the colony continues to expand.

  • · Mud tubes along walls or foundations
  • · Wood sounds hollow when tapped
Cockroach activity

Droppings and egg cases indicate pests are nesting nearby.

Cockroaches reproduce quickly and often hide in warm, damp areas close to food and water.

  • · Dark droppings inside cabinets
  • · Musty odor near kitchens or drains
Rodent activity

Scratching sounds and gnaw marks can mean rodents are inside.

Rats and mice contaminate surfaces, damage wiring, and use small openings to move through the property.

  • · Gnaw marks on packaging or wiring
  • · Noises inside ceilings and walls
Mosquito breeding

Standing water can turn into a breeding site within days.

Gutters, containers, drains, and shaded outdoor areas can support recurring mosquito activity around your home.

  • · Frequent bites around shaded areas
  • · Water collecting after rain

How much does pest-control service cost?

Pricing depends on the pest, property size, and level of activity. We inspect the affected areas and provide a clear quote before treatment begins.

Do I need to leave during treatment?

It depends on the treatment. We explain all preparation, safety precautions, and re-entry guidance before service so you know exactly what to expect.

How long does treatment take?

Many residential services can be completed in one visit. Larger or more active infestations may require additional treatment and follow-up.

Is pest-control treatment safe for my family?

We apply products carefully and provide clear preparation and aftercare directions. Tell us about children, pets, or sensitivities so the service plan can account for your household.

How quickly will I see results?

Results vary by pest and infestation level. Activity often drops after treatment, while termites, rodents, or established colonies may need monitoring and follow-up.

Can you inspect a property treated by another provider?

Absolutely. We can assess current activity, explain what is needed, and provide a clear quote before beginning any new treatment.
